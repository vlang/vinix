The no-sync host command is native V in
[`diskhost/no_sync.v`](../native-disk-tools/diskhost/no_sync.v). It owns archive
construction, the eight ordered epochs, debugfs capture/decoding and checks.
The original independent C guest fixture remains unchanged.

The directly owned QEMU process group is killed and reaped before printing
the raw ARM transcript or inspecting the volume. Complete inode numbers remain
decimal text, and the completion marker must include its newline. See
[qualification and retained guest failures](../native-disk-tools/README.md).
