Pinned Linux v6.12 kernel/exit.c kernel_wait4 calls put_user(wo.wo_stat, stat_addr) only after do_wait selects a child event. The native wrapper copies rusage only after a positive kernel_wait4 result. Identical-ELF Linux6.12.51 references on both architectures preserve all accessible bytes of stable one/two/three-byte status guard faults, permit valid cross-page stores, write exactly four status bytes, consume selected events on EFAULT, and ignore bad outputs on no-event/errors. Native patch resolves the full scalar range and both endpoints before its existing status copy; no claims, pins, accounting or cleanup changes. Concurrent mapping changes and general store atomicity remain outside the bounded contract.
