Pinned Linux v6.12 kernel/exit.c consumes selected stopped/continued state or reaps an exited child in do_wait before native user output. WNOWAIT bypasses consumption. Later status, rusage or siginfo EFAULT cannot restore the event. Identical-ELF Linux6.12.51 references qualify these observable rules. Native patch changes only four completion arguments and comments; existing claims, pins, helpers, copyouts and success paths remain identical. Independent lifetime review is retained. Completed-exit probes require pidfd POLLIN; concurrent waiters and incomplete exit publication remain outside the qualified contract.
