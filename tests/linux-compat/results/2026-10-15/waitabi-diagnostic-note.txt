The first probe incorrectly required unchanged waitid siginfo on errors and preservation of a zombie after wait4 copyout failure. Linux v6.12 instead writes the siginfo fields on these errors and consumes an exited child before wait4 status/rusage copyout. Both identical-ELF Linux references therefore failed, reached the expected ECHILD setup refusal at the later reap, and panicked on PID1 exit. These full original source/C/ELF/receipts/serial profiles remain pinned. Final p2 controls compare successful siginfo output plus exact selector/option errors, use held-child wait4 output faults (no event, no copyout), and require retention only for WNOWAIT waitid. Error siginfo copyout and consuming wait4 copy faults are published unsupported semantics, not silently treated as passing. Final sources/C/ELFs are independently equal across original/fixed/Linux.
