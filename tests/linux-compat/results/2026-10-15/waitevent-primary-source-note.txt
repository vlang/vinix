Pinned Linux v6.12 kernel/exit.c stores six child siginfo fields in order, preserving padding/tail and permitting a protected suffix beyond those fields. It checks the full siginfo numeric range before scalar stores. Stable split-field guard faults leave the incomplete field untouched in both identical-ELF Linux6.12.51 references. Native changes add a synchronous field-copy helper and use it for event results, preserving credentials/accounting order, wait claims, pins, ownership and consumption. Consuming faults and rusage-copy ordering remain separate.
