Immutable Linux v6.12 exit.c source is copied and hash checked from the prior wait qualification, then compared with unchanged native ELFs on Linux6.12.51. The waitid wrapper writes six scalar fields, skips padding, checks the full siginfo numeric range, and gives failed output access EFAULT priority over errors. Stable split-field guard faults leave their partial field untouched in both references. No child claim, pin, lock, lifetime or allocator is added by this fix.
